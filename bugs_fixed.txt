Bug 4 / Improvement: testbed_description/urdf/testbed.gazebo - Lidar max range too short (1.5 m)

- Issue: 
    AMCL pose drifted from the robot's true position while teleoping
    most scan rays were infinite, and the robot appeared far off in RViz than its actual location

- Cause: the sensor's <range><max> was 1.5 m, too short for the map.
    in open areas it returned no data, so AMCL could not correct odometry drift

- Fix: 
    set <max> to 8 m
    using 8m, several walls are visible and it is a realistic range for a 2D lidar
    laser scan now overlays the map walls in RViz, and stays aligned even after extended driving
